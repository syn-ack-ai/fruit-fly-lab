World

Closed loop: fruit that smells (wind-blown vinegar plume) and tastes (fermenting, ripe, mouldy), a garden's leafy background smell and wind. Every receptor neuron fires at its measured resting rate. Tasting sugar or bitter teaches the mushroom body. The senses drive real FlyWire neurons; the body moves only by descending-neuron output plus a simple walking/flight rhythm (the nerve cord is not in the connectome).

Stimuli

Each stimulus drives real FlyWire sensory neurons. Nothing here tells the fly what to do.

Move something toward the camera. Its measured size and expansion drive the same real LC4 + LPLC2 neurons as the rock. Sideways motion should not.

The pan/tilt camera becomes the fly's head. Moving things and people (detected on the AI HAT) drive the real looming (LC4, LPLC2) and pursuit (LC10a) neurons; the brain's steering neurons (DNa01/DNa02, DNp09) turn the camera. Vision is blanked while it turns, as flies do with an efference copy.

Drives food odour (266 ORNs), tarsal contact chemosensation (71 GRNs) and proboscis sugar (23 GRNs) together — what a fly meets when food is put in front of it.
